# Imagine classics — Drupal demo

A working demonstration built by Thaung Su Nyein in November 2026, for an application to a
UN DESA consultancy. It is a personal demo, not client or production work, and it is not
affiliated with or endorsed by the United Nations.

**Live:** https://cms.storyheroapp.com *(coming soon)*

## What it is

Public-domain classics from [Imagine by StoryHero](https://storyheroapp.com) — *Alice's Adventures
in Wonderland* in English, French and German, with narration and artwork — migrated into Drupal
by a custom module, running in Docker on an Azure VM behind Cloudflare. The site launches on
Drupal 10 and is upgraded in place to Drupal 11.

Full write-up, architecture and runbook to follow.
